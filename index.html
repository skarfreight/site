local, long-distance, & cross-border solutions

Skar Freight provides dependable logistics and transport services for cargo moving across Southern Africa and the wider SADC region. Since 2003, we have built a strong reputation for delivering local, long-distance, and cross-border freight solutions that are safe, secure, and consistently on time.

In-depth regional route knowledge, customs procedures & compliance, our experienced team ensures every shipment is handled efficiency with care.
From smaller consignments to oversized and abnormal loads, We offer complete end-to-end logistics support tailored to each client's needs.
Our focus on professionalism, reliability, punctual delivery allows businesses to transport their goods across borders with confidence and peace of mind.
